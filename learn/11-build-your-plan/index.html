Build your plan

Learning goal: turn the lessons into a written plan you can revisit.

1. Pull the pieces together

You now have the map: paycheck → budget → safety net → debt → accounts → investments → protection. The Plan Builder stores your numbers on this device and prints a one-pager.

2. Suggested checklist

  • Estimate take-home with the paycheck tool.
  • Set living costs and a savings transfer.
  • Capture employer match if offered.
  • Attack high-rate debt; refinance only with clear math.
  • Fund 3–6 months of essentials in an HYSA.
  • Choose Roth vs traditional with your tax picture in mind.
  • Automate a simple index or target-date investment.
  • Confirm beneficiaries and basic insurance.

3. Revisit yearly

Every January (or after a job change): update salary, check contribution limits, rebalance if needed, and reread the flow page.

Example

Open Plan Builder, fill the few required fields, then print. Also walk the paycheck flow once so the order sticks.

Key takeaways

  • A short written plan beats perfect theory.
  • Automate, then review yearly.
  • This site is educational — not personalized financial advice.
