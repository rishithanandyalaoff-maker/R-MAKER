Protection

Learning goal: cover the basics that keep a money plan from blowing up.

1. Health insurance

Employer plans usually beat going bare. Know your deductible, out-of-pocket max, and whether you have an HSA option (HSA eligibility has rules — confirm before contributing).

2. Other insurance worth knowing

  • Renters insurance — cheap protection for belongings and liability.
  • Disability insurance — protects your income if you cannot work.
  • Life insurance — mainly if someone depends on your income.

3. Beneficiaries

Retirement accounts and life insurance pay to the beneficiary you name — not automatically to a will. Update names when life changes.

4. Basic legal docs

As finances grow: a will, powers of attorney, and (later) more estate planning. Early-career priority is usually insurance + beneficiaries + a simple will if you have dependents or meaningful assets.

Watch out

This is not legal advice. Use it as a checklist of topics to raise with HR and, when needed, a lawyer.

Key takeaways

  • Insurance protects the plan; investing grows it.
  • Name beneficiaries on accounts.
  • Add legal docs as complexity grows.
