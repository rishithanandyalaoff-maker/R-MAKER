The flow

Order of operations for every paycheck. Tap a step to open the matching lesson or plan page.

  1. Step 1

    Before day one: cash for needs within 2 years; rest toward Roth IRA if you have earned income

  2. Step 2

    Paycheck arrives: taxes out; 401(k) at least up to the full match

  3. Step 3

    Living costs: fixed budget, ideally about 25–30% of gross for housing when possible

  4. Step 4

    Decision: debt above ~7%? Yes → pay it down first

  5. Step 5

    Emergency fund: 3–6 months (lean ~6 on a visa)

  6. Step 6

    Roth IRA: yearly limit; backdoor if income is high

  7. Step 7

    Decision: staying in the US 5+ more years? No → short-horizon path

  8. Step 8

    Max 401(k) and HSA when the path is long-horizon

  9. Step 9

    Brokerage: everything left

  10. Step 10

    Loop: next paycheck; review every January

Visa note

Short-horizon path: take employer free money, keep more in brokerage and cash, use a safer mix, and pay high-rate debt faster. Details in visa and cross-border.
