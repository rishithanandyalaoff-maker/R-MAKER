Myth busters

IRAs give lower returns

Same investments and tax treatment can produce the same results. Match, deductibility, and fees differ.

Pay off all debt before investing

High-rate debt first. Low-rate debt can coexist with investing, especially when free employer money is on the table.

You need a lot of money to start

Small, steady contributions matter early because of compounding and habit.

My tax bracket is my tax rate

Brackets are marginal. Your effective (average) rate is usually lower.

Moving abroad means cashing out my 401(k)

You often can leave money invested. Penalties depend on age and rules, not just location — get cross-border advice.

Roth is always better

Roth shines when today’s tax rate is low. Traditional can win when today’s rate is high.

Timing the market beats lump sum

Historically, lump sum wins often. Monthly investing can still be the plan that you will actually follow.
